## design and construction

Our experience in custom luxury home design and construction is evident at every step of our eight-stage design and construction process. The process includes key insights from our professional interior designers, guaranteeing your complete satisfaction. Our attention to detail ensures an excellent outcome.

#### 1. Initial contact stage

We meet with you to discuss your requirements, either onsite or in our office.

We provide a detailed explanation of the steps involved in the design and construction process.

#### 2. Preliminary stage

A preliminary agreement is prepared and signed.

You pay the initial(non-refundable) preliminary deposit based on your project.

We arrange a soil test, contour survey, property information reports and property inspection.

We prepare conceptual drawings, including, 3D external still images.  (3D videos additional fee)

#### 3. Pre-contract and selections stage

We prepare your new home Tender for presentation to you at our office.

Your Tender is signed, and you pay the second part of the preliminary deposit which is based on your project.

Colours, fittings & fixtures are selected with the support of our interior designer, this includes individual appointments at our supplier’s showrooms.

Full contract drawings are completed.

#### 4. Contract stage

We prepare your contract documents including drawings, specifications, engineering and HIA QC1 Construction Contract for presentation to you.

The Contract is signed and the Balance of 5% deposit (less previous amounts paid) is required.

#### 5. Approvals stage

We lodge your drawings with the building certifier and relevant council for standard building and plumbing approvals.

#### 6. Construction stage

Construction typically commences within 15 days after receipt of building, plumbing & finance approvals.

We provide the opportunity for you to attend regular meetings on site with the building supervisor, where any questions or queries you may have can be discussed.

#### 7. Completion and handover stage

Your new home is cleaned inside and out by a professional commercial cleaner.

The site will be cleared of all builders rubbish.

We meet with you on site to walk through your new home, present you with the keys and handover folder for your new home, containing all final certificates and operating manuals. We also provide you with a thank you gift for choosing Lindon Homes to bring your dream to life.

#### 8. New Home Care

It is important for us to continue our relationship, so we want you to know our service doesn’t end at completion. We will come back and inspect your home 12 months after handover.
